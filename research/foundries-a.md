Family index: each width variant is a huge line of its own name in its own cut, with a tiny outlined mono count pill ("12") at the end of the line. Below it, a grid of dark rounded cards (about 193x146, 8px gap, radius ~8, bg #111 on #252525), each with an outlined mono title pill and a stack of weights ("Hairline / Light / Regular / Bold / Black / Ultra", italic paired on the same line). The page ends the dark section with a white sheet that has big rounded top corners (~64px+) sliding up over it. Sticky pill nav (Families · Try · Features · Info · Glyphs · In Use · Trial fonts · Buy): black pills (#000, white text) for the primary, light-grey pills (#e6e6e6) for sections, ~41px high, centered. ## Ranked moves for Isoform (Specimen + Wall)

1. **Live tester = live figure with axis sliders** (Displaay). A dark block, 4 sliders in one row above a ~420px-tall figure: label 15px/0.3px tracking left, 1px white track at ~215px, 14px round thumb, numeric value at right. Axes for us: Speed, Draw (stroke progress), Accent on/off, Reach. The figure re-renders on drag. This is the headline interaction of the site.
2. **Style grid = figure family grid** (Pangram). One black sticky preview card (518x360, radius 16) + 3-column grid of cells `background:#ededed` (dark mode: `#14161a`), ~241x104, gap 12, radius 16; each cell shows the figure name left and a small accent-blue index/number right (14px bold, replaces their #ff2f00 weight number). Hover cell = loads that figure into the sticky card; `transition: opacity .6s cubic-bezier(.33,1,.68,1)`.
3. **Corner-bracket frames with caption** (Displaay). Each specimen sits inside a frame with 20x20 corner brackets (1px, 8px radius on the outer corner, inset 20px from the page edge) plus a centred 15px/0.3px-tracked caption ("Fig. 03 - Bolt, 7 strokes") and a small top-centre dash tick (~20px wide). Replaces plain borders; pairs well with the crosshair marks already planned.
4. **Weights stacked in one sentence = story steps** (Klim, Dinamo). Klim sets a bold word over a hairline word with a long dash at ~170px; Dinamo stacks "Hairline & Italic ... Ultra & Italic" in tiny rows. For us: show the same figure at 3 states (outline / mid-draw / finished with accent) stacked in one row inside each family card, labelled mono 11px caps.
5. **Wall tiles as rounded cards with a title pill** (Dinamo cards + Pangram radius). Dark tiles `#111` on `#252525`, ~193x146 base unit, radius 8-16, 8px gap; centred outlined mono pill title (1px, full radius, ~11px mono, truncated with ellipsis); a "12"-style count pill on the family index line. Gives the Wall its rhythm; vary a few tiles to 2x height for the tall effect.
6. **Sticky pill sub-nav with one filled primary** (Dinamo, Pangram). Centered, ~41px high pills: current section `#000`/white text (invert in dark: white fill/black text), others `#e6e6e6` (dark: `rgba(255,255,255,.1)`), primary CTA ("Get the skill") a solid accent-blue pill ~31-41px high, full radius, always at the far right; anchor links turn accent-coloured when active (Pangram). Matches Vercel's pill switcher already planned.
7. **Left label column + hairline rules** (Grilli, Klim). 128-267px left gutter holds 14-16px labels ("Rules", "Palette", "Axes"); content column starts at x=311 (Grilli) with full-width 1px rules (`#fff/0.15` dark) between blocks. Index lists = rows ~89px tall with name in its own style and 1px `#999` hairline, two columns, no cards. Use for the rules list / palette section instead of more cards.
8. **Disclosure rows + outlined download buttons** (Klim). 50px rows, 1px `#555` top rule, small triangle + 16px label (Technical details, Rules used, Licence); buttons `border:1px solid #555; border-radius:2px; padding:0 8px; height:36px` with trailing arrow glyph (right arrow, down arrow). Good for the install/spec block and "Download skill" without shouting.
9. **Full-viewport flat colour transitions / white sheet with large rounded top** (Klim, Commercial, Dinamo). Rhythm via ~378px flat colour bands (Commercial) or a white sheet sliding up with ~64px top radius over the dark section (Dinamo). For us: only 2-3 dark/blue-accent bands between Wall sections; caption each with a tiny centred line ("4 Families, 48 Styles" analogue: "12 figures, 3 icons each").
10. **Floating buy/CTA + pinned-state tag** (Grilli, Commercial). Fixed bottom-right black pill `height:40px;padding:1px 20px;border-radius:5px;transition:transform .3s` ("Use <figure>" / copy command), plus Commercial's tiny "pinned" tag when a figure is selected in the inspector. Keeps the CTA reachable while scrolling the Wall.

## Not worth taking
Dinamo's cookie/fortune-cookie gimmicks; Klim's pure-colour home (zero information); Commercial's default-HTML nav look; Pangram's photo cards (we have no photos, and the wall tiles already cover this).
